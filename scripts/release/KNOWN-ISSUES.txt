ROSES - known issues (release {RELEASE})
======================================

Duelists of the Roses is in beta: you will run into bugs and missing
pieces. These are the ones we already know about.

- A few rare rule situations are not supported yet.
  When one comes up, the game stops: the window title then reads
  "Roses - stopped: ..." with the reason. Close the game, start it
  again from the launcher and Continue from your last save. Please
  report it.
- Some loading times and fades differ slightly from the original.
- The intro/ending movies' sound sync has not been checked by ear.
- Two-player Custom Duel and Trade need a second controller (or the
  second keyboard layout) and have had little testing.
- Online Play is a beta: make an account on the launcher's Profile
  page (see README.txt). No rankings or chat yet, and it
  has had little testing between two PCs.
- In Build Deck the reincarnation and password screens and the 3D
  card viewer are not drawn yet: the card lists stay on screen. If the
  buttons seem to stop working there, press Circle or Cross.
- Nothing is saved automatically. Save on the campaign map with
  SYSTEM -> SAVE; "Continue" on the title screen loads it.


Reporting a problem
-------------------
Click "Report a problem" in the launcher. It opens roses-data\logs\.
Send launcher.log and game.log (and the "-previous" copies) with a
short description of what you were doing. The logs contain no game
data, only messages and folder paths on your PC.
